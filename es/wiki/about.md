# About Crate Delight

## Overview

Crate Delight is an add-on for Farmer's Delight, and a standalone mod, that expands Minecraft's item storage options by adding more crates and bags for different ingredients, food items, and materials that save space in your inventory. They're also useful to decorate any build in your world.
