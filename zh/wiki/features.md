# Features

## Produce Crates

**Included Blocks:**
Carrot Crate, Beetroot Crate, Potato Crate, Poisonous Potato Crate, Apple Crate, Berry Crate, Glow Berry Crate, Red Mushroom Crate, Brown Mushroom Crate, Pumpkin Slice Crate

**Crafting Recipe:**
Place 9 of the corresponding produce in a Crafting Table.

![Produce Crates](/assets/gifs/produce_crates.gif)
